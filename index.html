The Generation Of Women Who Did Everything Right

You were told that if you were excellent, the rest would follow.

You became excellent. Here is what actually happened.

  • 46% of women report burnout, compared with 37% of men.
  • There are now 1.6 million more women than men enrolled in four-year colleges — a larger gap than in 1972, in the opposite direction.
  • Only 31% of entry-level women have a sponsor, compared to 45% of men. Employees with sponsors are nearly twice as likely to be promoted.
  • At that first step up to manager, 93 women are promoted for every 100 men — a gap that has held for eleven consecutive years. For women of color, it is 74.
  • Women are 59% of college graduates in the United States and 48% of those entering the corporate workforce. The attrition starts before the first promotion.
  • 45% of college-educated single women say their relationship status comes down to not finding anyone who meets their expectations. It is the reason they cite most.

None of this is in your head, and none of it is your fault.

It is also not the whole story — because the numbers describe what is happening socially in society. They do not describe what happens inside a woman who has spent fifteen years or more adapting to it. That part is quieter, and it is the part nobody measures: the slow calibration of how much to want, how loudly to want it, and how much of yourself to bring.

Many women find a way to manage this through not seeing for real, denial, or rationalization. You are here, reading this because some part of you is done managing it and want to see reality for what it truly is.

What You Learned Before You Had Words For It

Underneath the symptom is almost always something older, hidden, unaccounted for, and unaddressed.

A mother whose approval would shift and change, so you learned to track it. A father who was absent, or present and unreachable, so you learned that being chosen was something you earned. A family where money meant fear, shame, or silence, or someone else’s permission. Being the capable one before you were old enough to choose it. Being told, directly or in a thousand small ways, that the full size of you was too much. Learning to recognize in someone’s eyes that your sexuality is what they wanted, not you.

And for many women — not all, but far more than will ever say so — something happened to your body, or around your body, before you had language for it. You handled it. You kept going. That is what makes it invisible.

You were a young girl as all this was happening. You did the only thing plausible to you at that time: you got good at reading the room, and you got excellent at everything else.

It worked. That is the difficult part. It worked so well that the adaptation became the personality.

And now you are the woman who reads every room accurately yet cannot locate her own wants inside it. The woman who is excellent at everything yet cannot ask for anything. The woman who manages her body yet cannot feel it.

The door is not permanently closed. It is an old adaptation running an operating system you never consciously chose.

Frequently Asked Questions

I’m not a spiritual person. Will this work for me?

Yes — arguably better. Skeptics get results here because nothing is asked of you on faith. You do not have to believe in energy work for your nervous system to release multiple years of held grief. Bring the skepticism. It is a form of integrity.

How is this different from other women’s groups?

Most women’s circles hold space. This one moves energy, light and consciousness. We, Women of Light™ keeps a structured balance between receiving new tools, practices and guidance, and having real time to share and be supported — live, in the moment, in a small circle of committed women. You are not there to be witnessed politely. You are there to transform for real.

Why only three months? Can we really accomplish all of this?

Quality, not quantity. Direct contact with the root — week after week, with trust, support and integration — moves more than years of talking about it. The twelve gateways work synergistically; move one and the others move with it.

I’ve done therapy for years. How is this different?

Therapy is largely conversational and works through insight. We, Women of Light™ works through the whole Self, in a group, with breakthrough and integration built in on every level. It is not a replacement for therapy and does not claim to be yet has proven that it fulfills what therapy and true soul awakening accomplish in tandem.

I’ve meditated for years. Isn’t this the same thing?

No, and this is worth being precise about. Meditation trains you to witness what arises. That is a genuine skill — and it can quietly become a way to observe your anger for a decade without once feeling it. The work we do in We, Women Of Light™ dissolves the charge in the body rather than watching it pass. Most women with a long meditation practice find this goes somewhere their practice never took them.

Will we use meditation or body practices during the sessions?

Yes. The heart of Iana’s work is transformation through whole bodymind and soul integration. Every session includes Spirit Gateways® BELIGHT MEDITATION® and LoveToBE the Movement® practices, energy work, and soul recalibration — to seat the change in your bodymind rather than your notes.

What if I don’t want to talk?

Then don’t. You are never required to share. Witnessing another woman’s work is real work — most women find their first breakthrough happens while someone else is in the chair and sometimes it is just their time— first time in the hot spot.

Are the group sessions confidential?

Every woman signs a confidentiality agreement. Personal work is never recorded. Replays cover Iana’s teaching only.

I’m carrying something I’ve never told anyone.

You would not be the first woman in this circle carrying that, and you will not be the last. There is no requirement to disclose anything, ever. You go at your own pace. Iana is deeply experienced with sexual wounding, family violence, abandonment, betrayal and rejection, love, loss, spiritual misguidance, divorce, marriage, higher sexuality, addictions, career fulfillment, true spiritual growth, karmic completions, and creative mastery. What this program does is move you toward your own light — it is not trauma treatment, and if you need clinical care it is not a substitute for it. Say so on your call and Iana will be straight with you.

Is it essential that I attend every session?

Don’t plan to miss. The container depends on the circle being whole. Life happens, and teaching replays exist — but a woman who plans to miss sessions should take a later cohort.

My schedule is tight. How much extra time does the workbook take?

About twice a week, ten to fifteen minutes each — part of whatever reflection time already works for you.

What happens after the twelve weeks?

You keep the workbook, the replays, the community, and — in most cohorts — the women connections. Many circles continue meeting on their own. That is a good sign, not a loss.
